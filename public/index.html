ABOUT THE ARTIST

Dion Dominic

I am a photojournalist and documentary filmmaker dedicated to capturing raw, unfiltered human experiences. For over a decade, I have traveled to the margins of society to document stories that challenge our perspectives and unite our shared humanity.

My work has been featured in leading editorial publications globally, focusing on environmental change, cultural preservation, and resilience.
